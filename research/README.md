# Research Framework

This directory contains the research framework for the iNFINITE AI 2025 Council Simulator.

## Structure

- `conference/` - Conference research results and findings
- `protocols/` - Protocol definitions and improvements
- `logs/` - Research log results and analysis
- `findings/` - Research findings and outcomes

## Purpose

This framework supports:
- Systematic research and documentation
- Conference research results management
- Protocol review and improvement
- Continuous improvement processes
